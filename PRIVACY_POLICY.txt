HI3 HASHKIT — PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 2026-09-09

Hi3 Hashkit ("the app") is a privacy-first, local-first Android application for
monitoring and controlling Bitcoin miners on your own network. This policy explains what
the app does and does not do with your data. In short: the app has no account, no ads, no
analytics, and no cloud of ours. Your data stays on your device unless you explicitly turn
on one of the optional integrations described below.


1. WHO WE ARE

Hi3 Hashkit is developed and published by the developer identified on the app's Google
Play listing. Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact address shown on that
listing.


2. THE SHORT VERSION

- We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
- There is no user account and no login to any service of ours.
- There are no analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or trackers in the app.
- All monitoring data (miners, telemetry history, settings, notes) is stored locally on
  your device.
- Nothing leaves your device except (a) requests to the miners you add, on your own local
  network or VPN, and (b) optional integrations that are OFF by default and only contact a
  destination you configure.


3. DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

The app stores the following locally, on your device only:

- The miners you add (name, network address, model, group/site, notes).
- Telemetry history (hashrate, temperatures, power, efficiency, shares, uptime, pool).
- Your settings and preferences.
- Optional per-miner maintenance notes and photos you add.
- Optionally, a miner's own admin password or API token, if you enter one to use a control
  feature. Such secrets are encrypted at rest using a non-exportable key in the Android
  Keystore (AES-256-GCM), decrypted only in memory to authenticate a request to that miner,
  and never logged, exported, or sent anywhere else.

This data is not backed up to the cloud by the app: Android's automatic backup is disabled
(allowBackup="false"). You can export data only through actions you start yourself (CSV,
diagnostics, or a JSON backup), which go to a destination you choose via the Android share
sheet.


4. WHAT CAN LEAVE YOUR DEVICE

a) Requests to your miners (core function).
   The app communicates with the miner addresses you add, over your local network (LAN) or
   your own VPN (e.g. Tailscale). These destinations must be private/local addresses; the
   app refuses to connect to public internet addresses for miner traffic. No third party is
   involved.

b) Optional integrations — all OFF by default, each enabled individually in Settings:

   - Network difficulty and Bitcoin price: a single read-only request to mempool.space to
     support solo-odds and profitability estimates. No miner data is sent.
   - Pool statistics: read-only requests to the mining pool you select (e.g. Hi3/Public
     Pool, CKPool, OCEAN, F2Pool, Braiins Pool), identified by your payout address,
     account, or a read-only token you provide. No miner telemetry or passwords are sent.
   - Hi3 MMP fleet view: read-only requests to a Mining Management Platform URL you
     configure, authenticated by an API key you provide (stored encrypted in the Android
     Keystore). Nothing is uploaded.
   - Firmware update check: at most one read-only request to GitHub to read the latest
     public firmware version tag. No device data is sent.
   - Alert webhook (push notifications): if you configure a push service (ntfy, Gotify,
     Telegram, or a generic URL), an active alert triggers one message to the endpoint YOU
     specify, containing the miner's name and the alert type/message only — no credentials,
     addresses, or telemetry beyond that.
   - MQTT publish (e.g. Home Assistant): if enabled, the app publishes a small fleet and
     per-miner telemetry summary to the local MQTT broker you configure. This is a local-
     network publish only.
   - Local web dashboard / metrics endpoint: if enabled, the app runs a small read-only
     web server on your local network so a browser or a Prometheus/Grafana instance can view
     fleet status. It serves data only; it accepts no input and offers no controls.
   - Paired Wear OS watch: if you install the companion watch app, a small fleet summary is
     sent to your paired watch over the local Wearable Data Layer (Bluetooth/Wi-Fi), not the
     internet.
   - Smart plug control/metering: if you configure a smart plug for a miner, the app sends
     on/off commands and reads power over your local network only.

We (the developer) do not receive any of this data. The app never uploads your miner data
to any server operated by us.


5. PERMISSIONS AND WHY THEY ARE USED

- Internet / Network state / Wi-Fi state: to reach and monitor miners on your LAN or VPN,
  and to detect the active network for scanning.
- Notifications: to show miner alerts you enable.
- Camera: used only, on demand, to scan a payout-address QR code. No image is stored or
  transmitted. The camera is optional.
- Foreground service (special use): only if you enable the optional always-on over-
  temperature safety cutoff, which watches a configured smart plug and cuts power if a
  miner overheats. It shows a persistent notification while active and does nothing else.
- Receive boot completed: only to re-arm that safety cutoff after a device restart, if you
  enabled it.

The app does not request location, contacts, microphone, or background-location
permissions.


6. DATA WE DO NOT COLLECT

- No personal identifiers, contacts, location, or advertising IDs.
- No usage analytics or crash telemetry sent to us or any third party.
- No selling or sharing of any data with third parties.


7. CHILDREN

The app is a technical tool for cryptocurrency-mining hardware and is not directed to
children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly
collect data from children.


8. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

Because data is stored only on your device, you control it entirely:
- Deleting a miner removes its stored data.
- Adjusting the retention setting prunes older telemetry.
- Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your device.


9. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The app does not embed third-party advertising or analytics services. Optional integrations
you choose to enable (a mining pool, mempool.space, GitHub, your push provider, your MQTT
broker) are operated by those third parties under their own privacy policies; the app only
contacts them when you enable and configure them, and only sends the minimal data described
in Section 4.


10. SECURITY

Secrets are encrypted at rest with the Android Keystore. Cleartext local connections to
miners are permitted only toward private/local network addresses, enforced in code. The app
has no account system and stores no credentials on any server.


11. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the
"Last updated" date above and in the app's release notes.


12. CONTACT

For privacy questions, contact the developer at the email address listed on the app's
Google Play Store page.
